Paris Travel Guide

Must-see attractions:
- Eiffel Tower: Iconic iron lattice tower, best viewed at sunset
- Louvre Museum: Home to the Mona Lisa and thousands of artworks
- Notre-Dame Cathedral: Gothic architecture masterpiece
- Champs-Élysées: Famous shopping avenue
- Montmartre: Historic hilltop neighborhood with Sacré-Cœur

Local Cuisine:
- Croissants and baguettes from local bakeries
- French onion soup
- Coq au vin
- Crème brûlée for dessert

Transportation:
- Metro system is extensive and easy to use
- Consider a Paris Visite pass for unlimited travel
- Walking is the best way to explore neighborhoods

Language Tips:
- Learn basic French phrases: "Bonjour", "Merci", "S'il vous plaît"
- Most people in tourist areas speak English
